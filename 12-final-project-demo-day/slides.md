# Judging & Completion Criteria Recap
## Straight from the Session 10 rubric

- **Line following:** robot detects and follows the taped line through the course
- **Obstacle handling:** robot detects the obstacle and executes an appropriate response (stop, go-around, or reroute — whichever behavior your team implemented)
- **Course completion:** robot reaches the end of the course reliably (partial credit for partial completion — we care about your engineering process, not just a perfect run)
- Two attempts allowed; best run counts
- This is pass/fail-style completion credit, not a competitive ranking — every team that meets the criteria succeeds

This isn't a competition for first place — everyone who meets these three criteria passes the final project."

# Running Order & Format
## How today works

- Teams demo in the order posted on the board (drawn randomly this morning)
- Each team gets **~5 minutes**: set robot on the start line, run the course, brief Q&A from classmates/instructor
- While waiting: watch, take notes, and prepare one question to ask each team
- If a run fails, no problem — reset and use your second attempt later in the rotation
- Instructor will call up the next team ~1 minute before it's their turn — be ready!

# Course Wrap-Up: Your Sense–Plan–Act Journey
## From Session 1 to today

- Session 1-3: **Sense** — reading sensors (line sensor, ultrasonic distance sensor), understanding inputs
- Session 4-7: **Plan** — writing MicroPython logic, conditionals, control loops, motor control basics
- Session 8-11: **Act** — combining sensing + planning into real behaviors: line following, obstacle avoidance, and the full final challenge
- Session 12 (today): everything comes together on one course, in front of your peers
- You didn't just learn robotics — you practiced the engineering loop: build, test, debug, repeat
